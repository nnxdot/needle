Help

Questions, answered.

How to install Needle, add your music, and fix the most common problems. If your answer is not here, write to dot@nnx.fyi.

Installing

Which download do I need?
The installer is best for most people. It adds Needle to the Start menu and to "Open with" for music files, and it needs no administrator rights. The portable zip runs from any folder, with nothing installed.
Windows says "Windows protected your PC"
Windows shows this for apps it does not know yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway. The SHA-256 checksums let you check that your download is the one we published.
Can I install it with winget?
Yes, once Needle is in the winget catalogue: winget install nnxdot.Needle.
How do I update?
Needle checks for a new version once a day and tells you when one is out. Install it from Settings › Online services. Needle 1.0.0 cannot find updates by itself; install a newer version from this site once.
How do I uninstall?
In Windows Settings, open Apps › Installed apps, find Needle, and choose Uninstall. Your library and history stay in %LOCALAPPDATA%\nnx\Needle\data, in case you come back. Delete that folder to remove them.

Your music

How do I add music?
Choose a folder in the welcome guide, or in Settings › Library. Needle reads the songs in it and watches it, so new music appears by itself.
Which formats can Needle play?
FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, MP3, AAC, Ogg Vorbis, Opus, WavPack, Monkey's Audio, DSD (DSF), and CUE sheets. Dolby Digital and Dolby Atmos music in M4A or MP4 files (such as Atmos downloads from Apple Music) plays as stereo; Atmos height sound is not played.
Will Needle change or move my files?
Only when you ask, for example when you edit tags or use Fix my library. Before it changes a file's tags, Needle keeps a backup copy, and moves in Fix my library can be undone.
Where are lyrics and covers?
Needle always uses covers, .lrc files, and lyrics tags that are with your music. To look up missing ones online, turn on Look things up online in Settings › Library. See the privacy policy for what is sent.
How do I back up my library?
Use Settings › Your data › Back up. It saves your history, ratings, and playlists. It does not copy your music files.
Can Needle play music from my Navidrome server?
Yes, and from other Subsonic servers such as Airsonic and Gonic. In Settings › Plugins, press Set up, then type your server's address, user name, and password. Its songs show with your own, under Servers in the sidebar, and stream as they play. Plays and ratings are saved on the server too. To keep a song as a file, right-click it and choose Save to my music.
Can I change Needle's colors?
Yes. In Settings › Appearance, choose a look and press Make a copy, then change any color. To add a theme someone sent you, drop its file on the Needle window. See Themes to write one by hand.

Sound

Where is the equalizer?
Choose the equalizer button next to the volume, or open Settings › Sound. Choose Graphic for ten sliders, or Parametric to set each band's kind, frequency, gain, and width. Save as preset keeps your own settings.
Can I use a correction for my headphones?
Yes. Download your headphones' ParametricEQ.txt from AutoEq, and choose Load EQ file…. Files from Equalizer APO work too.
What are effects?
Effects, such as a room sound or an echo, come from plugins. Add the example plugins in Settings › Plugins, turn them on, and add effects in Sound › Effects. See Plugins to write your own.
Why do the equalizer and effects do nothing?
Exclusive output is probably on. It sends each file to your sound device unchanged, so the equalizer and effects are skipped. Turn it off in Settings › Playback.

Speakers

How do I play on a speaker?
Choose the speaker button in the player bar. Needle finds Chromecast, AirPlay, and DLNA speakers on your network. The first sound can take a few seconds.
Can I play on several speakers at once?
Yes. In the same menu, choose Play on several at once…, pick the speakers (and this computer, if you like), and choose Play on these. Needle lines them up. If one sounds early or late, move its slider in Settings › Playback › Speaker timing.
Needle does not find my speaker
Check that the computer and the speaker are on the same network, that no VPN is on, and that Windows lets Needle use private networks. Guest Wi-Fi networks often keep devices apart. AirPlay 2-only speakers are not supported yet.

Phone remote

How do I control Needle from my phone?
Turn on Phone remote in Settings › Playback, then point your phone's camera at the code. The page shows what is playing, lets you play, pause, skip, change the volume, pick from Up next, and find music. Add it to your home screen to open it like an app.
The page does not open on my phone
The phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the computer, and not on a guest network. If Windows asked about Needle and the network, allow private networks. Needle must stay open on the computer.
Is it safe?
The address holds a long secret, and Needle answers only devices on private networks, such as your Wi-Fi or a VPN you use. Anyone with the address who can reach your computer that way can control the music, so share it only with people you trust. New address locks out every phone that had the old one.

When something goes wrong

There is no sound
Check the volume in Needle and in Windows, and which output is chosen in the speaker menu and in Settings › Playback. If a speaker stops answering, Needle goes back to this computer.
Needle crashed
Open Needle again. If crash reports are on, the report is sent then, without file paths or your name. See the privacy policy.
How do I report a problem?
In Settings › Your data, choose Copy error report. It copies the recent log without file paths or your name. Paste it in an email to dot@nnx.fyi, and say what you did just before. Open log folder shows the full log.
Can Needle keep playing with its window closed?
Yes. Turn on Hide to tray in Settings › Playback. Closing the window then hides Needle by the clock while the music plays on. Click its icon to bring it back, or right-click it to play, pause, skip, or quit.
How do I see the welcome guide again?
Settings › Your data › Welcome guide.
