Questions, answered.

How to install Needle, add your music, and fix the most common problems. If your answer is not here, write to dot@nnx.fyi.

Installing

Which download do I need?
On Windows, the installer is best for most people. It adds Needle to the Start menu and to "Open with" for music files, and it needs no administrator rights. The portable zip runs from any folder, with nothing installed. On Linux, take the .deb for Ubuntu, Linux Mint, and Debian, or the .rpm for Fedora and openSUSE. On a Mac, use the Terminal command below.
How do I install it on a Mac?
Needle runs on Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 15 or newer. Open Terminal and paste curl -fsSL https://needle.nnx.fyi/install-mac.sh | bash. It downloads Needle, checks it against the published checksums, puts it in Applications, and opens it. You can also download the disk image and drag Needle to Applications. Needle is signed without an Apple developer account, so the first time you open a copy from the disk image macOS stops it: open System Settings › Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway. The first time Needle saves a password, and once after each update, macOS asks to let it use your keychain: choose Always Allow.
How do I install it on Linux?
Open the downloaded file, and your software center installs it; or in a terminal, sudo apt install ./needle_*_amd64.deb on Ubuntu and Mint, or sudo dnf install ./needle-*.x86_64.rpm on Fedora. Needle then appears with your other apps. It needs Ubuntu 24.04, Linux Mint 22, Debian 13, Fedora 40, or newer. For the tray icon on GNOME, add the AppIndicator extension. Dolby Atmos music plays with FFmpeg, which the package asks for.
How do I install it on an iPhone?
Needle for iPhone is not in the App Store. It installs through SideStore or AltStore, free apps that install other apps with your own Apple ID. Install one of them, then add Needle's source, https://needle.nnx.fyi/sidestore.json, and install Needle from it. It needs iOS 26 or newer. With a free Apple ID, apps installed this way stop opening after 7 days unless they are refreshed; SideStore and AltStore refresh them for you. Updates come through the same source.
Windows says "Windows protected your PC"
Windows shows this for apps it does not know yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway. The SHA-256 checksums let you check that your download is the one we published.
Can I install it with winget?
Yes, once Needle is in the winget catalogue: winget install nnxdot.Needle.
How do I update?
Needle checks for a new version once a day and tells you when one is out. Press Update to in Settings › Online services. On Linux, your system asks for your password once, as for any other update. On a Mac, Needle replaces itself in Applications; macOS asks once again to let it use your keychain. After an update, Needle shows what is new. Needle 1.0.0 cannot find updates by itself; install a newer version from this site once.
How do I uninstall?
In Windows Settings, open Apps › Installed apps, find Needle, and choose Uninstall. Your library and history stay in %LOCALAPPDATA%\nnx\Needle\data, in case you come back. Delete that folder to remove them. On Linux, remove the needle package with your software center, apt, or dnf; your library stays in ~/.local/share/needle. On a Mac, drag Needle from Applications to the Trash; your library stays in ~/Library/Application Support/studio.nnx.Needle.

Your music

How do I add music?
Choose a folder in the welcome guide, or in Settings › Library. Needle reads the songs in it and watches it, so new music appears by itself.
Which formats can Needle play?
FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, MP3, AAC, Ogg Vorbis, Opus, WavPack, Monkey's Audio, DSD (DSF), and CUE sheets. Dolby Digital and Dolby Atmos music in M4A or MP4 files (such as Atmos downloads from Apple Music) plays as stereo; Atmos height sound is not played.
Will Needle change or move my files?
Only when you ask, for example when you edit tags or use Fix my library. Before it changes a file's tags, Needle keeps a backup copy, and moves in Fix my library can be undone.
Where are lyrics and covers?
Needle always uses covers, .lrc files, and lyrics tags that are with your music. To look up missing ones online, turn on Look things up online in Settings › Library. For more timed lyrics, especially for K-pop, J-pop, and C-pop, press Also look on NetEase where a song has none, or turn on NetEase lyrics in Settings › Plugins. The lyrics button in the player bar (or Ctrl+L) shows them beside your library, and F11 shows them across the whole screen. See the privacy policy for what is sent.
Can album covers move?
Yes. Put an animated WebP or GIF beside an album's songs, named cover.webp or animated.gif for example, and it moves in the big player and in full screen. On a Mac, Android, and iPhone, a video such as cover.mp4 works too. The folder must hold only that album. Albums on your music server use the server's own moving cover. Turn it off in Settings › Appearance › Moving covers; with Reduce motion on, covers keep still. On a phone, Needle downloads no new moving covers on mobile data unless you turn on On mobile data, under Moving covers.
How do I make a playlist?
Press the plus beside Playlists in the sidebar. Give it a name, a description if you like, and a cover: a picture of your own, or its songs' covers. Choose Songs I pick and add songs with Add songs, from any song's menu, or by dragging them onto the playlist; or choose Smart playlist and pick its rules from menus, such as Rating is at least 4. Needle counts the songs that match as you go. Drag songs to put a playlist in your own order.
How do I back up my library?
Use Settings › Your data › Back up. It saves your history, ratings, and playlists. It does not copy your music files.
Can Needle play music from my Navidrome server?
Yes, and from other Subsonic servers such as Airsonic and Gonic. They are built in, with no plugin to add. In Settings › Library › Music servers, press Sign in… and type your server's address, user name, and password. On Android, open Settings › Music servers; on an iPhone, Settings › Music server. Needle uses https unless you type http:// in front; use plain http (Navidrome on port 4533 often needs it) only at home or through a VPN, because it is not encrypted. Its songs show with your own, with a small server icon beside the artist (turn it off in Settings › Appearance › Mark server songs), and stream as they play. Plays and ratings are saved on the server too. Update now gets new songs. Refresh everything gets every song and cover again, and removes songs the server no longer has. If the network sleeps, a song waits and goes on from where it stopped. With octo-fiesta in front of your server, turn on Search the server as you type (experimental) under your server in Settings › Library › Music servers: songs it can fetch show above your search results. To play songs without the network, right-click them and choose Keep on this computer, or press the pin on an album's page. To keep a song as a file in your music folder, choose Save to my music.
Can I lay Needle out like Apple Music?
Yes. In Settings › Appearance › Layout, set The player to Top bar: the controls, the song playing, and the volume move into the title bar. Set A folded sidebar to Icons, and folding the sidebar (Ctrl+B) leaves a strip of page icons instead of hiding it. Set The search field to Ctrl+K to leave search to the command palette (Ctrl+K or Ctrl+F). Your mouse's side buttons go back and forward between pages.
Can I change Needle's colors?
Yes. In Settings › Appearance, choose a look and press Make a copy, then change any color. To add a theme someone sent you, drop its file on the Needle window. See Themes to write one by hand.

Sound

Where is the equalizer?
Choose the equalizer button next to the volume, or open Settings › Sound. Choose Graphic for ten sliders, or Parametric to set each band's kind, frequency, gain, and width. Save as preset keeps your own settings.
Can I use a correction for my headphones?
Yes. Download your headphones' ParametricEQ.txt from AutoEq, and choose Load EQ file…. Files from Equalizer APO work too.
What are effects?
Effects, such as a room sound or an echo, come from plugins. Add the example plugins in Settings › Plugins, turn them on, and add effects in Sound › Effects. See Plugins to write your own.
Why do the equalizer and effects do nothing?
Exclusive output is probably on. It sends each file to your sound device unchanged, so the equalizer and effects are skipped. Turn it off in Settings › Playback.

Speakers

How do I play on a speaker?
Choose the speaker button in the player bar. Needle finds Chromecast, AirPlay, and DLNA speakers on your network. The first sound can take a few seconds.
Can I play on several speakers at once?
Yes. In the same menu, choose Play on several at once…, pick the speakers (and this computer, if you like), and choose Play on these. Needle lines them up. If one sounds early or late, move its slider in Settings › Playback › Speaker timing.
Needle does not find my speaker
Check that the computer and the speaker are on the same network, that no VPN is on, and that Windows lets Needle use private networks. Guest Wi-Fi networks often keep devices apart. AirPlay 2-only speakers are not supported yet.

Phone remote

How do I control Needle from my phone?
Turn on Phone remote in Settings › Playback, then point your phone's camera at the code. The page shows what is playing, lets you play, pause, skip, change the volume, pick from Up next, and find music. Add it to your home screen to open it like an app.
The page does not open on my phone
The phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the computer, and not on a guest network. If Windows asked about Needle and the network, allow private networks. Needle must stay open on the computer.
Is it safe?
The address holds a long secret, and Needle answers only devices on private networks, such as your Wi-Fi or a VPN you use. Anyone with the address who can reach your computer that way can control the music, so share it only with people you trust. New address locks out every phone that had the old one.

When something goes wrong

There is no sound
Check the volume in Needle and in Windows, and which output is chosen in the speaker menu and in Settings › Playback. If a speaker stops answering, Needle goes back to this computer.
Needle crashed
Open Needle again. If crash reports are on, the report is sent then, without file paths or your name. See the privacy policy.
How do I report a problem?
In Settings › Your data, choose Copy error report. It copies the recent log without file paths or your name. Paste it in an email to dot@nnx.fyi, and say what you did just before. Open log folder shows the full log.
Can Needle keep playing with its window closed?
Yes. Turn on Hide to tray in Settings › Playback. Closing the window then hides Needle by the clock while the music plays on. Click its icon to bring it back, or right-click it to play, pause, skip, or quit.
How do I see the welcome guide again?
Settings › Your data › Welcome guide.
