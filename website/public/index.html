Your system's media controls work with Needle. Plugins add commands, sound effects, and music sources.

Yours stays yours.

  • No account.

    Needle works without one, and keeps your library on your computer.

  • Online lookups stay off until you turn them on.

    Then Needle sends only the artist, album, and title, to find lyrics and covers.

  • Your files stay as they are.

    Needle moves or changes a file only when you ask. When you do, it keeps a backup.

  • One question a day.

    Needle asks whether a newer version is out. You can turn that off in Settings.

Needle for Mac

Version 1.7.1. Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 11 or newer.

Paste this into Terminal, then press Return:

curl -fsSL https://needle.nnx.fyi/install-mac.sh | bash

It downloads Needle, checks it against the published checksum, and puts it in Applications.

Or the disk image (.dmg)

Needle is not signed by Apple, so macOS holds back a downloaded disk image the first time. Help explains how to open it. 52 MB

Needle for iPhone

Version 1.7.1. iOS 26 and newer.

Needle for iPhone is not in the App Store. Install SideStore or AltStore first, then open this page on your iPhone:

Add to SideStoreAdd to AltStore

Or add the source by hand: https://needle.nnx.fyi/sidestore.json. SideStore and AltStore sign Needle with your own Apple ID, so a free one works; Needle then needs a refresh every 7 days, which they do for you. The .ipa file 14 MB
