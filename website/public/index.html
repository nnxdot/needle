Privacy

Yours stays yours.

  • No account.

    Needle works without one, and keeps your library on your computer.

  • Lookups stay off until you turn them on.

    Then Needle sends only the artist, album, and title, to find lyrics and covers.

  • Your files stay as they are.

    Needle changes a file only when you ask, and keeps a backup when it does.

  • One question a day.

    Needle asks whether a newer version is out. You can turn that off.

Needle for Mac

Version 1.8.0 · Apple silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 15 or newer

Paste this into Terminal, then press Return:

curl -fsSL https://needle.nnx.fyi/install-mac.sh | bash

It downloads Needle, checks it against the published checksum, and puts it in Applications.

Or the disk image (.dmg)

Needle is not signed by Apple, so macOS holds back a downloaded disk image the first time. Help explains how to open it. 53 MB

Needle for Android

Version 1.8.0 · Android 8.0 and newer

For most phonesFor Intel and AMD devices

Most phones and tablets need the first one (ARM64). The second (x86_64) is for devices with Intel or AMD chips, and for emulators. Open the downloaded file to install it. Older TVs with a 32-bit chip need the 32-bit one (ARMv7). 36 MB each.

Version 1.8.0 · iOS 26 and newer
