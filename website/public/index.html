Yours stays yours.

Needle works without an account and keeps your library on your computer. Looking up lyrics and covers online is off until you turn it on, and then it sends only the artist, album, and title.

Your files are never moved or changed unless you ask. When you do, Needle keeps a backup.

Once a day, Needle asks for news of a newer version. You can turn that off in Settings.
