Privacy policy

Your music stays yours.

Needle is a music player that runs on your computer. It has no account, no ads, and no tracking. This page lists everything Needle can send over the internet, and when.

Last changed 23 September 2026. Applies to Needle 1.2.0 and later.

The short version

  • Your library, playlists, ratings, listening history, and settings are stored only on your computer, in Needle's data folder.
  • Needle does not collect statistics about how you use it, and it has no ads.
  • Needle goes online only for the things listed below. Most are off until you turn them on.
  • We (NNX, who make Needle) receive only two things: the update check and, if Needle crashes, a crash report. You can turn both off.

What reaches us

Update checks

Once a day, Needle downloads needle.nnx.fyi/latest.json to see if a newer version is out. Like any web request, this shows our server your IP address. We do not keep a list of who asked. Turn it off in Settings › Online services.

Crash reports

If Needle crashes, it saves a report on your computer. The next time Needle starts, it sends the report to needle.nnx.fyi. A report holds:

  • the Needle version, and "windows x86_64" (or your system and chip type)
  • the error message, and where in Needle's code the crash happened

Before sending, Needle takes out every file path, your Windows user name, and your computer's name. A report says nothing about your music or what you listen to. We use reports only to find and fix bugs, and we delete them after 12 months.

Turn crash reports off in Settings › Your data, or in the welcome guide. When they are off, reports stay on your computer and are deleted after 30 days. Copy error report in the same place copies a report you can read before you share it.

Email

If you write to dot@nnx.fyi, we use your email only to answer you.

On your own network

  • Speakers. To find Chromecast, AirPlay, and DLNA speakers, Needle asks the devices on your network. When you play on a speaker, Needle sends it the sound, and the song's name and cover.
  • Phone remote. When you turn it on, Needle answers devices on private networks (your own Wi-Fi, or a VPN you use) that have its address. The address holds a long secret, and New address locks out every phone that had the old one. It is off until you turn it on.

Your data

Needle's data folder is %LOCALAPPDATA%\nnx\Needle\data. It holds the library database, covers, backups of tags Needle changed, plugins, and logs. The log file is logs\needle.log. It stays on your computer unless you share it. Uninstalling Needle leaves this folder, so you do not lose your history; delete it to remove everything.

Passwords and keys for Last.fm, ListenBrainz, and AcoustID are kept in Windows Credential Manager, not in Needle's files.
