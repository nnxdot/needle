# 2.0.0
A new Home and icon, and a lot more to see and hear.
! **A new look.** Home is the Mac's: the greeting, the song playing now with a round Play button, then your albums and artists on shelves. Needle's colour is amber, its words are the Mac's, and its icon is new: Glass groove, an amber record of glass on charcoal.
- [player] **Choose the player's buttons.** Put them in order, take some out, or add Shuffle and Repeat in Settings › Appearance › Player buttons.
- [palette] **Fonts.** Choose a font for titles (eight of them, such as Fraunces, Poster, and Instrument Serif) and one for everything else (such as Inter and Atkinson Hyperlegible), or a font file from Files, in Settings › Appearance › Titles and Text.
- [search] **A new Search.** The best match large, then songs, albums, artists, and lines of lyrics that hold the words, with a chip for each and rules to try.
- [player] **Four looks for the player.** The Look button at its top steps through Cover, Record (the cover as a turning record), Lyrics (only the words, very large, lit as they are sung), and Visualizer.
- [player] **iPhone Duo.** Open it and Needle spreads out: the tabs stand at the right side, and the player puts the cover on one side of the fold and its controls on the other. Close it and it is the phone you know.
- [player] **Sideways.** Turn your iPhone and Needle turns with it: the cover on one side, the controls on the other.
- [sound] **The visualizer.** Light that moves with the music, in the cover's colours: the player's Visualizer look, or over the whole screen from the player's ••• menu.
- [player] **Motion.** An album's cover grows into its page, a page's name folds into the bar as you scroll, and the colours follow the music. With Reduce motion, only fades.
- [player] **A seek bar shaped like the song.** The player's seek bar shows the song's loud and quiet parts. Or choose Wave, Line, or Thick in Settings › Appearance › Seek bar.
- [lyrics] **Lyrics in your language.** Tap the translate button beside Lyrics, and each line shows translated under it, made on your iPhone by Apple's Translation.
- [lyrics] **Lyric cards.** Hold a line of the lyrics and choose Share as Card for a picture of it in the cover's colours.
- [artists] **Better artists.** Guests after "feat." and both halves of "A & B" get their own page, sort names from your tags are used, and accents sort where they belong. Sort artists by A–Z, Most played, Most songs, or Newest, show only album artists, and jump with the letters at the side.
- [artists] **Your own artist pictures.** The picture button on an artist's page lets you choose their photo, wide picture, and logo: one from your photos, or any of fanart.tv's (logos on grey, so white ones and black ones both show). A picture you choose shows at once, as it is.
- [globe] **Pictures for every artist.** With online lookups on, Needle finds every artist's pictures slowly in the background (on Wi-Fi), or all at once with Check all now (Settings › Library › Artist pictures).
- [history] **On this day.** Home shows what you played one and five years ago today.
- [albums] **Your library in numbers.** What your music is made of, in Library › Library in numbers.
- [radio] **The genre map.** Every genre as a bubble placed by how its songs sound: tap one to play it, shuffled (Radio › Genre map).
- [albums] **A record crate.** Flip through your albums like records in a box: tap the crate at the top of Albums.
- [player] **Moving covers on the album page.** An album with a moving cover shows it moving on its page too.
- [sound] **Smart mixes.** With crossfade on, the fade starts where the music ends and lasts whole bars (Settings › Playback › Smart mixes).
- [moon] **A sleep timer.** The moon at the foot of the player stops the music after 15, 30, or 45 minutes, an hour, or at the end of the song or the album, fading out over the last minute. It stays lit while it runs, with the time left beside it and on the Lock Screen.
- [speed] **Speed and pitch.** From the player's ••• menu: play a song from half to twice as fast, keep its pitch or let it rise, and move it up or down in semitones, for every song or only this one.
- [history] **Your year in music.** Your hours, plays, and new songs, the year by month, your top artist, and your top songs, a card at a time, from Listening history (and on Home in December). Save it as a picture, make a playlist of it, or play your top 50.
- [lyrics] **Time the lyrics.** −0.2 s, +0.2 s, Redo line, and Undo fix the line you just tapped; Time Each Word in the ••• menu times each word.
- [albums] **What makes an album.** Songs without an album artist but tagged as a compilation are one album by Various Artists, and with Folder, a folder's songs with one album name are one album. Settings › Library › Albums; your files never change.

# 1.8.0
Navidrome built in, a Radio tab, moving covers, and calmer Settings.
! **Navidrome and Subsonic, built in.** Sign in to your music server in Settings › Music server, with no plugin to add. Its songs show with your own, with a small server icon beside the artist (turn it off in Settings › Appearance › Mark server songs). Long lists load faster.
- [server] **A page for your server.** It shows how the server is, its songs, albums, and artists, and when it was last updated. Update now gets new songs. Refresh everything gets every song and cover again and removes songs the server no longer has. The page also shows what is kept on this iPhone, with Free Space and Stop Keeping All.
- [player] **Moving covers.** An animated WebP or GIF beside an album's songs (cover.webp, animated.gif), or the album's moving cover on your music server, moves in the player: in the immersive cover, the square cover, and the record. Turn it off in Settings › Appearance › Moving covers.
- [signal] **Moving covers on mobile data.** On mobile data, Needle does not download new moving covers. The ones it has still play. To get new ones too, turn on Settings › Appearance › On mobile data, under Moving covers.
- [radio] **A Radio tab.** Radio takes the place of Sources: your station from the songs you play most, quick mixes, and a station for each song you play most. Sources is now in Settings.
- [albums] **A new Home.** Home greets you, with quick picks, a line under each shelf, your week, and the artists you play most.
- [palette] **Calmer Settings.** Every setting is a row with a small icon and one short line. What's new and About are buttons at the top of Settings. Sign-in forms open only when you press Connect or Sign in.
- [lyrics] **Lyrics under the mini player.** The line being sung shows in the mini player, in place of the artist. Turn it on in Settings › Appearance › Lyrics under the mini player.
- [player] **Music that keeps playing.** When the network sleeps (on a locked screen, say), a song from your server waits and goes on from where it stopped. If the server does not answer for 5 minutes, the song pauses at its place, and Play goes on from there.
- [account] **Last.fm in one click.** Press Connect in Settings › Scrobbling, and Last.fm's sign-in page opens. You no longer fill anything in.
- What's new and the welcome screen are neater: each change and each choice is a row with an icon.
- A cover changed on your server comes in with Refresh everything.
- Stars you give the playing song show at once.
- Artist pictures from fanart.tv are the newest by when the photo was taken.

# 1.7.1
Fixes for covers and crash reports.
- Covers that are not square (a tall Blu-ray cover, a wide one) fill their tile, the same size as the rest.
- With crash reports on, Needle also sends the crashes iOS tells it of, not only the ones Needle catches itself. They tell what went wrong and where in the app, with no names and no files.

# 1.7.0
Albums and artists after Apple Music, a player you can sink into, and a long list of fixes.
! **Album and artist pages, after Apple Music.** An album's cover runs across the top and fades into a page in its own colours. An artist's page opens on a tall photo of them, with their logo where fanart.tv has one, a large Play between Shuffle and Radio, their latest release, and their top songs.
- [player] **An immersive player.** The cover fills the top of the player, edge to edge, and moving covers (a cover.mp4 beside the songs) play there. Turn either off in Settings › Appearance.
- [player] **Controls after Apple Music.** Plain buttons, a thin volume line between two speakers, and Up Next with wide Shuffle and Repeat buttons. Tap the artist's name to open their page.
- [lyrics] **Lyrics that keep up.** Lyrics from your server line up with the song, a song's own words come before a translation, and lyrics you time show at once.
- [discord] **Discord, smoother.** Unlinking clears your status at once, and you can cancel while linking.
- Opening Needle no longer stops another app's sound: Needle takes the sound only when you play.
- Crash reports are your choice on the welcome screen, and nothing goes online unless you ask. Check for a newer Needle in Settings › About.
- Play on an artist's page plays all their songs, and Play and Shuffle on Songs take every song.
- A song whose file is gone is left off its album's page, and a tap on one says so instead of opening an empty player.
- Removing a music folder asks first.
- The A–B loop ends when another song plays.
- The star and Favourites follow a rating at once.
- VoiceOver can move the seek bar and the volume, and names the player's buttons.
- Many smaller fixes for widgets, the sleep timer, the computer link, and your music server.

# 1.6.5
Discord status, and lyrics from your music server.
! **Discord status.** Link your Discord account once in Settings › Discord, and your profile shows what you play, as on Android and the desktop.
- [lyrics] **Lyrics from your server.** Songs from Navidrome get the lyrics your server has, timed when it has them, even with online lookups off.
- When a song has no lyrics and online lookups are off, the lyrics page has a button to turn them on.
- Long lyric lines no longer run past the edge of the screen.

# 1.6.4
Needle comes to iPhone, made for Liquid Glass.
! **Needle for iPhone.** Your library, music servers, and Needle on your computer, in an app made for iOS 26: a glass tab bar with the song playing on it, a full player over the cover's colours, and lyrics that follow the song.
- [player] **Lock screen and Control Center.** Music plays on with the screen off, pauses for calls and when headphones come out, and plays on AirPlay, Bluetooth, Chromecast, and DLNA speakers.
- [widget] **Widgets.** The song playing, albums played lately, playlists, and your week of listening, on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen; play and pause from Control Center, and ask Siri to play your favourites or your radio.
- [computer] **Your computer.** Scan the phone remote's QR code to work Needle on the computer, search its music, play it here, and move what plays from one to the other.
- [sound] **Sound.** The ten-band and parametric equalizer, headphone corrections, crossfeed, crossfade, and even loudness, as on the desktop.
- [lyrics] **Lyrics.** Word-by-word karaoke, lyrics from NetEase, and timing your own by tapping along.
- [playlist] **Playlists and tools.** Make and edit playlists and smart playlists, edit tags, find duplicates and covers, tidy the files, and bring in a sync file from the computer.
- A sleep timer counts down on the Lock Screen, and a server's songs coming in show as a Live Activity.
