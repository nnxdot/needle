# 2.0.0 [windows, linux]
A new look, after the Mac's, and a lot more to see and hear.
! **A new look.** The player floats over the foot of the page as one rounded bar, with a small screen for the song. The queue, lyrics, and details float beside the page. The page is a sheet with one round corner, the search field sits at the top of the sidebar, and Back, Forward, and the command palette share one small group.
- [moon] **A sleep timer.** The moon in the player stops the music after 15, 30, or 45 minutes, an hour, or at the end of the song or the album, fading out over the last minute. It stays lit while it runs and shows the time left under the pointer. Then sleep the PC, if you like.
- [speed] **Speed and pitch.** Play a song from half to twice as fast, keep its pitch or let it rise, and move it up or down in semitones, for every song or only this one. Exclusive output turns it off.
- [history] **Your year in music.** Your hours, plays, and new songs, the year by month, your top artist, and your top songs, from Listening history (and on Home in December). Save it as a picture in Pictures › Needle, make a playlist of it, or play your top 50.
- [lyrics] **Time the lyrics.** −0.2 s, +0.2 s, and Redo line fix the line you just tapped.
- [keyboard] **Keys that work everywhere.** Play, pause, skip, favorite, open the small player, and change the volume from any app, with your own keys, in Settings › Keyboard. Keys another app already uses show a warning. On Linux this works with X11; on Wayland, set keys in your desktop's settings.
- [play] **Buttons on the taskbar.** On Windows, hover Needle on the taskbar for Previous, Play/Pause, Next, and Favorite under its preview.
- [mini] **A small floating player.** A small card that stays above your other windows: the cover, the song, and the buttons under the pointer. Drag it anywhere; double-click it to open Needle. Open it from the command palette (Ctrl+K) or with your own key.
- [play] **Choose the player's buttons.** Drag buttons into the player, along it, and out of it in Settings › Appearance › Layout › Player buttons.
- [tag] **Fonts.** Choose a font for titles (eight of them, such as Fraunces, Poster, and Instrument Serif) and one for everything else (such as Inter and Atkinson Hyperlegible), or a font you have installed, in Settings › Appearance.
- [wave] **The visualizer.** Bars that move with the music, in its colours, under the cover, with a glow that breathes with it. Turn it on with the new button in the big player.
- [albums] **Gentler motion.** Pages rise as they open, album and playlist pages fold their header as you scroll, and covers lift with a glow in their own colour under the pointer. Reduce motion turns it off.
- [albums] **Looks for the big player.** The Look button steps through the cover with the lyrics beside it, the cover as a turning record, only the words very large, and the visualizer filling the space.
- [signal] **A seek bar shaped like the song.** In the big player and full screen, the seek bar shows the song's loud and quiet parts.
- [artists] **Better artists.** Guests after "feat." and both halves of "A & B" get their own page, sort names from your tags are used, and accents sort where they belong. Sort artists by name, plays, songs, or newest, show only album artists, and jump with the A–Z bar.
- [edit] **Your own artist pictures.** The pencil on an artist's page lets you choose their photo, wide picture, and logo: your own file, or any of fanart.tv's. A photo in an artist's folder (artist.jpg) is used too, and so are a wide picture and a logo there (fanart.jpg, logo.png) when fanart.tv has none or online lookups are off.
- [globe] **Pictures for every artist.** With online lookups on, Needle finds every artist's pictures slowly in the background, or all at once with Check all now (Settings › Artwork and lyrics).
- [history] **On this day.** Home shows what you played one and five years ago today.
- [albums] **Your library in numbers, and the mood map.** From Listening history: what your music is made of, and every song by how it sounds; click a spot to play the songs around it.
- [albums] **A record crate.** Flip through your albums like records in a box (Albums › Crate).
- [lyrics] **Lyric cards.** Right-click a line of the lyrics for a picture of it to share.
- [queue] **The queue in full screen.** A queue button beside the lyrics button.
- [search] **A Search page.** Type on Home or any page that is not a list: the best match comes first, large, then songs, albums, artists, and the lines of lyrics that hold your words. Rules to try are one click away.
- [play] **Moving covers on the album page.** An album with a moving cover shows it moving at the top of its page too.
- [eq] **Smart mixes.** With crossfade on, the fade starts where the music ends and lasts whole bars.
- Plugins can translate lyrics: a Translate button then shows under them.

A photo in an artist's folder (artist.jpg) is used too, and so are a wide picture and a logo there (fanart.jpg, logo.png) when fanart.tv has none or online lookups are off.

- [history] **On this day.** Home shows what you played one and five years ago today.
- [albums] **Your library in numbers, and the mood map.** In the sidebar: what your music is made of, and every song by how it sounds; drag a circle over the map to play the songs in it.
- [albums] **A record crate.** Flip through your albums like records in a box: choose the crate at the top of Albums, and drag or press the arrow keys.
- [queue] **The queue in full screen.** A queue button beside the lyrics button, and queue rows light up under the pointer.
- [play] **Moving covers on the album page.** An album with a moving cover shows it moving on its page too.
- [eq] **Smart mixes.** With crossfade on, the fade starts where the music ends and lasts whole bars (Settings › Playback › Smart mixes).

# 1.8.0 [macos]
Navidrome built in, moving covers, and calmer Settings.
! **Navidrome and Subsonic, built in.** Sign in to your music server in Settings › Library › Music servers, with no plugin to add. Its songs show with your own, with a small server icon on them (turn it off in Settings › Appearance › Mark server songs). Long lists load faster.
- [server] **A page for your server.** When you are signed in, it shows how the server is, its songs, albums, and artists, and when it was last updated. Update now gets new songs. Refresh everything gets every song and cover again and removes songs the server no longer has. The page also shows what is kept on this Mac, with Free Space and Stop Keeping All.
- [play] **Moving covers.** An animated WebP or GIF beside an album's songs (cover.webp, animated.gif), or the album's moving cover on your music server, moves in the big player and in full screen, in the immersive cover and in the square cover. Turn it off in Settings › Appearance › Moving covers.
- [palette] **Calmer Settings.** Every setting is a row with a small icon and one short line, as in System Settings. In Settings › Appearance, you choose the look and the background by picture, and where things sit by small sketches. Sign-in forms open only when you press Connect or Sign in.
- [signal] **Music that keeps playing.** When the network sleeps, a song from your server waits and goes on from where it stopped. If the server does not answer for 5 minutes, the song pauses at its place, and Play goes on from there.
- [lyrics] **Lyrics under the player.** The line being sung shows in a thin strip under the player. Turn it on in Settings › Appearance › Lyrics under the player.
- [fullscreen] **A bigger immersive cover.** When no side panel is open, the cover in the big player and in full screen fills the window at any size.
- [account] **Last.fm in one click.** Press Connect in Settings › Online services, and Last.fm's sign-in page opens. You no longer fill anything in.
- After an update, the Mac asks once to let Needle use the Keychain, not once for each sign-in.
- A cover changed on your server comes in with Refresh everything.
- Rows on Home scroll sideways with arrows.
- A click outside the search field, or Esc, ends typing in it. The big player takes the keys when it opens.
- The page scrolls on under the floating player.
- Artist pictures from fanart.tv are the newest by when the photo was taken.

# 1.6.2
Fixes for your files, saved settings, and everyday listening.
- [lyrics] **Safer file tidying.** Existing lyrics are left intact, and Needle tells you which files could not move. If Undo cannot put a file back, it stays available so you can clear the obstruction and try again.
- [eq] **Settings that stay saved.** Equalizer changes no longer erase new presets, bring back deleted ones, or revert other preferences. Tag edits also keep your measured loudness; replacing the audio clears measurements that no longer apply.
- [playlist] **More reliable CUE albums.** Different recordings no longer share an identity just because their file sizes and track timings match. Rescanning marks removed tracks as missing, and exported M3U playlists can be brought back with their CUE tracks and repeated entries in order.
- [cast] **Moving music between phone and computer.** Song matching uses the title and artist separately, and streamed songs keep their format. If the computer does not have the current song, Needle tells you before moving playback.
- On Linux, clicking the player artwork opens the big player instead of showing a window-resize cursor.
- Covers refresh when their picture changes at the same file path, without restarting Needle.
- Searches return small result sets faster in large libraries. Album and artist summaries now respect limits such as one song per artist or album.
- File tidying stays inside your selected music folders, leaving similarly named sibling folders alone.
- Unicode credit lines no longer panic during import, and unsupported DSF headers return an error before seeking.
- Incomplete speaker-discovery replies no longer crash the parser or discard other valid speakers.
- Queued listens for a disabled or signed-out service no longer hold up another listening service.

# 1.6.1
Needle gets faster and calmer, and you can lay it out your way.
- [globe] **Needle for Mac.** Needle now runs on Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer), with the menu bar icon, media keys, Now Playing, and your passwords in the Keychain. Get it from needle.nnx.fyi.
! **Faster everywhere.** Needle redraws only what changes, so it uses a fraction of the processor it did: pages at rest redraw 7 to 15 times a second instead of 40 to 70, and a start uses about a sixth of the work. Large covers are drawn from small copies, which saves memory and smooths scrolling.
- [panel] **Player in the top bar.** Turn it on in Settings › Appearance: the controls, the song playing, and the volume sit in the title bar, as in Apple Music, and the search field moves to the bottom.
- [menu] **Compact sidebar.** Also in Settings › Appearance: folding the sidebar (Ctrl+B) leaves a strip of page icons.
- [chevron-left] **Mouse back and forward buttons.** The side buttons of your mouse go back and forward between pages, and Alt+Right goes forward.
- [globe] **Search your server as you type (experimental).** For octo-fiesta: the Navidrome / Subsonic plugin can ask your server while you search, and songs it can fetch show above your results. Turn it on under your server in Settings › Plugins.
- Playlists in the sidebar show their picture, or a mosaic of their covers.
- The search field can be hidden in Settings › Appearance; Ctrl+F then opens the command palette.
- A music server that does not answer no longer freezes Play, Pause, and Next.
- Your music server's song list is refreshed on start only when it is over 6 hours old, a little after Needle opens.
- [mini] **Cover view in the mini player.** Point at the cover and press its button: the cover fills the mini player, with the song and the controls over it. The song is centred, as in Apple Music.
- With the player in the top bar, dragging the seek bar or the volume no longer keeps them stuck to the pointer.
- A short window keeps the sidebar's Settings, Import, and Fix my library in reach: the rest of the sidebar scrolls.
- The mini player's lyrics and queue buttons are as large as its other buttons.

# 1.5.0
Needle comes to Linux, and your music can fill the whole screen.
! **Needle for Linux.** Needle now runs on Linux as well as Windows: Ubuntu, Fedora, Mint, and more. Everything you know is there, from lyrics and themes to plugins and your music server, with media keys, the tray icon, Discord status, and your keyring. It draws its own window and needs no GTK.
- [fullscreen] **Full screen player.** Press F11, or the full screen button in the big player. The cover drifts behind large synced lyrics, and the controls step aside while you listen. Esc brings you back.
- [lyrics] **Lyrics beside your library.** The new lyrics button in the player bar (or Ctrl+L) opens the lyrics next to your songs. They follow the song, and a click on a line jumps there.
- [playlist] **Playlists, made properly.** A new playlist window gives each one a name, a description, and a cover: a picture of your own, or its songs' covers. Smart playlists are built from menus, such as Genre is K-pop and Played not in the last 30 days, and count the songs that match as you go. Drag songs into the order you want, and add more with Add songs.
- [plugin] **More places to find lyrics.** Plugins can now find lyrics too. The new NetEase lyrics plugin finds timed lyrics for a great many songs, K-pop and J-pop above all. Turn it on in Settings › Plugins, or press Also look on NetEase where a song has none.
- Songs in Dolby Digital Plus (Dolby Atmos) now show their sample rate and channels.
- Names in other scripts, such as Korean, no longer turn into "…" in Songs.
- Error messages no longer say the same thing twice.
